Frequently Asked Questions

General
What is AWS Student Community Day?
AWS Student Community Day Thane 2026 is a one-day community-driven event organized by the AWS Student Builder Group at APSIT. It brings together 400+ students, speakers, and industry professionals to learn, network, and explore cloud computing with AWS.
Who can attend?
The event is open to all engineering students, tech enthusiasts, developers, and anyone interested in cloud computing — regardless of their experience level. Beginners are very welcome!
Is registration free?
Please check the registration page on KonfHub for the latest pricing details. Some tickets may be free or subsidized for students. Register early as seats are limited.
Can beginners attend?
Absolutely! Sessions are available at 100 (Foundational), 200 (Intermediate), and 300 (Advanced) levels. There's something for everyone, whether you're just getting started with AWS or already building on the cloud.
Event Day
What should I bring?
Bring your registration confirmation (email or QR code), a valid college ID, a notebook or laptop if you'd like to take notes, and a water bottle. Comfortable clothes are recommended.
What is the event schedule?
Registration opens at 8:00 AM and the event begins at 9:00 AM on 3 October 2026 (Saturday). The full schedule will be published closer to the event date.
Will certificates be provided?
Yes, attendance certificates will be provided to all registered participants who attend the event. Details on how to claim them will be shared on the day.
Is food included?
Yes! Lunch and refreshments will be provided to all registered attendees. Dietary restrictions can be raised during registration or by contacting us in advance.
Is parking available?
Limited parking may be available at or near Kriyan Banquet Hall. We recommend using public transport or cab services to avoid any inconvenience.
Can I network with speakers and sponsors?
Yes! Networking is a key part of the event. There will be dedicated networking sessions and breaks where you can interact with speakers, sponsors, and fellow attendees.
What if I reach late?
You can still enter and attend sessions after the event starts, but we strongly recommend arriving by 8:30 AM to complete registration smoothly and not miss the opening keynote.
Travel & Venue
How do I reach the venue?
The venue is Kriyan Banquet Hall, Ghodbunder Road, Kasarvadavali, Thane West. It is approximately 10–12 km from Thane Station. Visit our Venue & Travel page for detailed directions.
Which transport is recommended?
Taking an Auto or Cab from Thane Station is the most recommended option. Ask for Ghodbunder Naka or Kasarvadavali Naka — the venue is a 3–5 minute walk from there. Avoid buses due to rush hour congestion.
What time should I arrive?
Registration opens at 8:00 AM. We recommend arriving by 8:30 AM to complete registration comfortably before the event begins at 9:00 AM.
