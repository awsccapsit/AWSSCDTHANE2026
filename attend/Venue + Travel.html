How to Reach
From Thane Station Recommended
  • Take an Auto or Cab from Thane Station.
  • Ask for Ghodbunder Naka or Kasarvadavali Naka.
  • Venue is a 3–5 minute walk from there.
By Bus Not Recommended
  • Avoid buses due to rush hour congestion.
