About the Event

AWS Student Community Day Thane 2026

AWS Student Community Day Thane 2026 is the first-ever official AWS Student Community Day in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, organized by the AWS Student Builder Group at A.P. Shah Institute of Technology (APSIT).

Set to take place on 3rd October 2026 at Kriyan Banquet, Thane West, this flagship full-day event is built with one core philosophy —

"For Students, By Students"

This is not just another college event. It is a professional-grade, high-energy technical gathering designed to give students a real taste of AWS Community Day and AWS Summit experience at a student level. The event aims to bridge the gap between classroom learning and industry expectations by bringing together 400+ passionate engineering students, industry experts, and leading tech companies under one roof.

What Makes This Event Special?
10+ insightful sessions covering Cloud Computing, DevOps, AI/ML, Cloud Native, Open Source, Startup Building, and Career Guidance
Inspiring keynote and panel discussions
Direct interaction with sponsors and industry professionals
Hands-on learning opportunities
Premium networking experience with fellow tech enthusiasts
Exclusive swags, delicious food, and memorable moments
Your Opportunity

AWS Student Community Day Thane 2026 is a platform where curious minds come together to learn, grow, network, and take their first steps toward building a successful career in cloud and emerging technologies.

This is your chance to be part of Thane's biggest student-driven event of the year.
